<h2 align="center">AAFUGO</h2>
<p align="center"><i>Session Export for Reaper</i></p>

AAFUGO hands a REAPER session to the mixer: AAF for Pro Tools, Nuendo/Cubase, Logic and Resolve, or DAWproject – with a Pre-Flight check, non-audio items sorted onto their own tracks and a Flight Report.

### Download
- **Mac** (Apple Silicon and Intel): `AAFUGO-VERSION-mac.dmg` below
- **Windows** (64-bit): `AAFUGO-VERSION-win64.exe` below

### Install
1. Quit REAPER first.
2. **Mac:** open the .dmg and double-click **Install AAFUGO**. **Windows:** open the installer. Click through; it puts AAFUGO into the REAPER UserPlugins folder.
3. Start REAPER and open **Extensions → AAFUGO**.

### Uninstall
- **Mac:** quit REAPER, open the .dmg and double-click **Uninstall AAFUGO** (also below as `AAFUGO-VERSION-mac-uninstall.pkg`).
- **Windows:** quit REAPER, open `AAFUGO-VERSION-win64-uninstall.exe` and click through. Or **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → AAFUGO → Uninstall**.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Mac: "Install AAFUGO can't be opened"**
>
> The installer is free and not signed by Apple, so macOS asks once:
> 1. Click **Done** in the warning.
> 2. Double-click **Blocked? Open Privacy & Security** in the .dmg window, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
> 3. Double-click **Install AAFUGO** again and choose **Open**.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Windows: "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen)**
>
> The installer is not signed by Microsoft.
> 1. Click **More info**.
> 2. Click **Run anyway**.
>
> If your browser blocks the download, choose **Keep** in the download list.
